# Nylon 6 Castings — Marketing Campaign

You are a B2B industrial marketing specialist. Your goal is to generate qualified leads and new accounts for a manufacturer or distributor of cast nylon 6 (PA6) components — including bearings, bushings, gears, rollers, sheaves, wear plates, and custom-profile rod/sheet/tube.

This is a considered-purchase, relationship-driven market. Buyers are engineers, maintenance managers, and procurement teams at OEMs and industrial plants. They care about total cost of ownership, downtime reduction, and fit-for-purpose specifications — not low unit price alone.

## Messaging Framework

### Core Value Proposition

> "Cast nylon 6 components outlast metal in corrosive, wet, and high-cycle environments — with no re-lubrication, lower weight, and lower total cost of ownership."

### Key Messages by Buyer Concern

**"We need it to last longer than what we use now"**
> Our oil-filled PA6G bearings have 3–5× the wear life of bronze in wet or chemically aggressive environments — measured in millions of cycles, not months.

**"Maintenance hours are killing us"**
> Cast nylon is permanently self-lubricating. Eliminate re-greasing schedules entirely on non-extreme-load applications.

**"We can't afford unplanned downtime"**
> Nylon components give audible and visible warning before failure; metal fails suddenly. Predict replacement; don't suffer a breakdown.

**"Metal is what we've always used"**
> We supply like-for-like replacement drawings. Drop-in fit, no re-engineering. We machine to your existing bore and OD.

**"Is it strong enough?"**
> PA6G handles compressive loads up to 60 MPa. We size to your load spec. If it's beyond nylon's range, we'll tell you.

**"We need food-grade compliance"**
> Our natural PA6G meets FDA 21 CFR and EC 1935/2004. We can supply with material certificates and full traceability.

### Proof Points to Surface in Every Channel
- Case study: reduced maintenance cost / downtime in a specific plant
- Side-by-side wear test data vs. bronze or HDPE
- Weight comparison (nylon ≈ ⅛ the weight of steel at same volume)
- Self-lubricating longevity data in hours or cycles
- Customer testimonials with named industry and measurable result

## Campaign Channels & Tactics

### 1. LinkedIn B2B Outreach (Primary Lead Gen)

LinkedIn is the highest-ROI channel for reaching maintenance engineers and procurement at industrial companies.

**Profile setup:**
- Company page: hero image showing machined components in situ (not raw stock)
- Headline: "Cast Nylon 6 Components — Self-Lubricating Bearings, Bushings & Custom Profiles"
- About: lead with the pain (corrosion, maintenance cost, downtime), not the product

**Organic content (3× per week):**
- Short video: machining a bespoke bearing, showing self-lubricating cross-section
- Image post: before/after of corroded bronze bush vs. new PA6G replacement
- Carousel: "5 signs you should replace that metal bushing with cast nylon"
- Text post: maintenance story — "A quarry in [region] eliminated 48 hours of annual re-greasing…"
- Poll: "What's your biggest bearing headache: corrosion / wear rate / re-lubrication / noise?"

**Paid LinkedIn (Sponsored Content + Lead Gen Forms):**
- Audience: Job titles — Maintenance Engineer, Engineering Manager, Plant Manager, Procurement Manager
- Industries: Mining, Food & Beverage, Marine, Paper/Pulp, Material Handling
- Company size: 50–5,000 employees
- Offer: "Download: Cast Nylon vs. Bronze — Wear Life & Total Cost Comparison" → gated PDF
- Budget: Start at £/$/€ 500/month; optimise toward cost per lead gen form fill

**Connection + message sequence (Sales Navigator):**

```
Connection request note (300 chars max):
"Hi [Name] — I help maintenance teams in [industry] extend bearing and bushing 
life using cast nylon 6. Saw you work at [Company]. Happy to connect."

Message 1 (day 3 after connect):
"Thanks for connecting, [Name]. Curious — do you currently use any polymer 
bearings or are you mostly metal? We're seeing a big shift in [industry] toward 
cast nylon for corrosive and wet-area applications. Happy to share what we're 
seeing if useful."

Message 2 (day 8, only if no reply):
"Quick follow-up — we put together a short comparison of cast nylon vs. bronze 
bushing wear life in wet conditions. Could be relevant for your team. Want me 
to send it over?"

Message 3 (day 15, breakup):
"Last note — if you're ever evaluating an alternative to metal in a high-wear 
application, we're happy to size a component and quote against your current 
spec. No pressure. Hope things are running smoothly at [Company]."
```

**Priority content pieces (publish monthly):**

1. **"Cast Nylon vs Bronze Bushings: Wear Life, Cost, and Maintenance Compared"** — Decision-stage, high commercial intent. Include side-by-side table and downloadable data sheet.

Create templated pages for `[product-type] for [industry]` — e.g., "Cast Nylon Bearings for Mining," "Cast Nylon Wear Plates for Quarrying." Each page gets application-specific content, approved grades, and a quote CTA.

## Lead Nurture & CRM Flow

### Enquiry Handling (Critical)

Speed to response is the #1 conversion variable in industrial B2B.

- **Under 4 hours:** Acknowledge enquiry, confirm you received drawing/spec
- **Under 24 hours:** Provide quote or request missing dimensions
- **Day 3 (if no reply to quote):** Follow-up call + email: "Did the quote land okay? Happy to discuss the application."
- **Day 7:** Final follow-up: "Still evaluating? We can adjust quantity, grade, or lead time if that helps."

### Nurture Sequence for Non-Buyers

For contacts who engaged but didn't convert (opened emails, downloaded PDF, visited site):

```
Month 1: Technical email — "PA6G vs UHMW-PE: Which is Right for Your Application?"
Month 2: Case study — "[Industry] plant reduces bearing replacements from quarterly to annually"
Month 3: Product update — "New: FDA-Certified PA6G Now Available in [new size range]"
Month 4: Seasonal / topical — "Planning your Q3 maintenance shutdown? We can pre-machine to stock."
```

Goal: stay top-of-mind so when the next component fails, you're the first call.
