Corrosion-proof

Unaffected by seawater, chemicals, acids, and alkalis. The go-to material for marine, water treatment, and chemical plant applications.

⚙️

Self-lubricating

Oil-impregnated PA6G releases lubricant under load. Eliminate re-greasing schedules and the failure risk that comes when maintenance is missed.

🔩

Drop-in replacement

We machine to your existing drawing. Match your bore, OD, keyway, and flange exactly — no re-engineering on your end.

Predictable failure

Nylon wears gradually and visibly. You get warning before failure. Metal fails suddenly. The difference is unplanned downtime vs. scheduled replacement.

Electrically insulating
